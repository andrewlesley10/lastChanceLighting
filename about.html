The Genesis

Our Story

Since 1998, we have been providing our customers with customised lighting — designed and supplied to match each client's unique requirements.

Last Chance Lighting (Pvt) Ltd, founded in 1998, specializes in project lighting solutions, lighting appliances, and home appliances for clients across Sri Lanka.

We are a modern, Colombo-based company operating at the heart of the island's lighting industry. Over the years, we have grown a loyal clientele and built a strong presence in Sri Lanka's most demanding sectors — including shipping, education, insurance, financial and banking, hospitality, and residential homeowners.

With decades of hands-on experience serving the local market, we are proud to enjoy the respect and trust of both our partners and our clients throughout Sri Lanka.

Our Story

Started With One Small Shop on Galle Road.

In 1998, Last Chance Lighting opened as a small family-run shop selling bulbs, fittings and the odd chandelier to neighbours in Colombo 03. Word travelled the way it does here — through relatives, contractors, and people who liked what their neighbour had put up.

Nearly thirty years on, we're still on Galle Road, still family-run, and still happiest when someone comes in undecided and leaves knowing exactly what they need.

Along the Way
